# Co-investments and Financial Relationships: gumi Cryptos Capital and Animoca Brands

Both investment firms share a formal partnership structure and have co-invested in at least six verified Web3 companies, with their most significant joint investments being **Yield Guild Games** and **OpenSea** during the 2019-2021 crypto gaming boom. The firms are also General Partners together in the **Decima Fund**, a ~$50M Japan-focused Web3 fund established in March 2024.

## Verified co-investments with documentation

Research confirms **six co-investment relationships** between gumi Cryptos Capital (gCC) and Animoca Brands, with varying levels of documentation quality suitable for legal purposes.

### Yield Guild Games — seed round co-investment confirmed

Both firms invested in YGG's **$1.325 million seed round** announced March 2021, led by Delphi Digital. This is documented in YGG's official Medium announcement, which lists both "gumi Cryptos Capital" and "Animoca Brands" among 15+ seed investors including BlockTower Capital, Scalar Capital, Polygon (then Matic), and Sfermion.

- **Round size:** $1.325M total
- **Date:** Funds raised late 2020, announced March 2021
- **Lead investor:** Delphi Digital
- **Individual amounts:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Documentation:** YGG Medium official announcement; gCC blog investment thesis; Animoca Medium "Why we invested in YGG"

Animoca Brands also participated in YGG's subsequent **$4M Series A** (June 2021, led by BITKRAFT Ventures), while gCC did not follow on. Both VentureBeat and TechCrunch articles about gCC's Fund II closure reference "seed-stage investment into YGG" as a notable gCC deal.

### OpenSea — November 2019 strategic round

Both firms co-invested in OpenSea's **$2.1 million strategic investment round** on November 19, 2019. gCC is documented as the lead investor for this round. OpenSea's official blog post "Bringing on Additional Strategic Investors" explicitly names both "gumi Cryptos" and "Animoca Brands" alongside Stanford StartX, 1Confirmation, and Blockchain Capital.

- **Round size:** $2.1M
- **Date:** November 19, 2019
- **Lead investor:** gumi Cryptos Capital
- **Documentation:** OpenSea official blog; Crunchbase funding data; Global Venturing (confirms "Coinbase, Animoca Brands and Gumi as backers")

OpenSea subsequently reached a **$13.3 billion valuation** in January 2022, making this one of gCC's most successful investments.

### Cyan — co-led seed round

Both firms **co-led** Cyan's **$2 million seed round** in 2022. Cyan provides Buy-Now-Pay-Later services for NFT purchases. This deal is significant because it demonstrates active collaboration, not just parallel participation.

- **Round size:** $2M
- **Date:** 2022
- **Lead investors:** gumi Cryptos Capital AND Animoca Brands (co-led)
- **Other investors:** OpenSea, Fabric Ventures, The Sandbox (angels), Yield Guild Games (angels)
- **Documentation:** Cyan Medium announcement; TokenInsight news article

### Particle Network — overlapping investment participation

Both firms invested in Particle Network, though in different rounds. gCC **co-led the $15M Series A** (June 2024) with Spartan Group. Animoca Ventures was an earlier backer, participating in the company's $7M raise. Particle Network's official blog explicitly states these investors "join our OG backers, which include Animoca Ventures."

- **gCC round:** $15M Series A (June 2024), co-led with Spartan Group
- **Animoca Ventures round:** $7M earlier raise
- **Documentation:** Particle Network official blog; TokenInsight

**Evans Huangfu**, now a Partner at gCC, previously invested in Particle Network while at Huobi Ventures, creating additional relationship connectivity.

### Amulet Protocol and Cookie3

Two additional co-investments have supporting documentation:

**Amulet Protocol** (DeFi insurance): gCC led the **$6M seed round**, with Animoca Brands listed as an investor in crypto news coverage.

**Cookie3** (MarketingFi): Animoca Brands led the **$2.5M strategic round** (2024), while Miko Matsumura (gCC Managing Partner) participated as an angel investor.

## Formal partnership structures

### The Decima Fund — joint General Partner status

The most significant formal relationship is the **Decima Fund**, a ~$50M (¥4.5 billion) Japan-focused Web3 fund where **gumi Inc.** (gCC's parent company) and **Animoca Brands** both serve as General Partners alongside MZ Cryptos (Yusaku Maezawa) and SBI Holdings.

- **Fund size:** Approximately ¥4.5 billion (~$50M USD)
- **Closing date:** March 14, 2024
- **Purpose:** Incubating domestic Web3 projects targeting overseas expansion
- **Investments made:** 10 investments completed as of March 2024

Yat Siu (Animoca co-founder) stated in the official announcement: "The mission of the Decima Fund aligns with our company's mission. I am proud to join the Decima Fund as a member, working alongside an experienced and trusted team."

This establishes a **structural relationship at the parent company level**, with both entities sharing fiduciary responsibility as fund managers.

### HECO Gaming Accelerator — founding advisors (July 2021)

On July 26, 2021, Huobi Eco Chain announced both Animoca Brands and gumi Cryptos Capital as **"founding advisors"** for the HECO Gaming Accelerator alongside Yield Guild Games. The PR Newswire press release explicitly names both firms as co-advisors providing "investment, incubation, development, distribution, marketing, and customer support" to blockchain game developers.

## Key personnel relationships

### Evans Huangfu — direct Animoca investment history

**Evans Huangfu** joined gumi Cryptos Capital as Partner in April 2024. Before joining gCC, he served as Partner & CIO at Arcane Group where he **invested directly in Animoca Brands** as a portfolio company.

From gCC's official press release announcing his hire: "His strategic investments included major industry players like **Animoca Brands**, Xterio, Fusionist, Particle Network and Pyth Network."

Additionally, while Head of Investment at Huobi Ventures, Huangfu's unit "committed to funds including Animoca Brands."

### Miko Matsumura — co-investment pattern, no formal role

**Miko Matsumura** (gCC Managing Partner) has co-invested alongside Animoca in multiple deals (Cookie3 angel investment, YGG, OpenSea) and has appeared at events alongside Animoca executives. However, **no evidence was found** of Miko Matsumura holding a formal board seat, advisory role, or employment relationship with Animoca Brands.

### Conference and media co-appearances

Miko Matsumura and Robby Yung (Animoca CEO) appeared together at Next Block Expo Berlin discussing crypto markets. Matsumura was also featured on Gravitaslabs (an Animoca partner company) podcast discussing Web3 adoption.

## Animoca Brands and Yield Guild Games relationship structure

Animoca's relationship with YGG extends beyond the seed investment to include an extensive strategic partnership:

- **Seed investment:** Part of $1.325M round (March 2021)
- **Series A follow-on:** Part of $4M round led by BITKRAFT (June 2021)
- **Memorandum of Understanding:** Signed November 14, 2022 with Animoca Ventures for co-investment collaboration, data sharing, and portfolio company growth strategies
- **REVV Racing YGG Cup:** $150,000 prize pool tournament (September 2021)
- **Anichess partnership:** YGG distributes Animoca's chess game in Southeast Asia (October 2024)
- **Guild Accelerator Program:** $30M joint program with Brinc

Yat Siu has stated publicly: "I've known Gabby Dizon, co-founder of YGG, for a long time. We share the same values, the same mission."

No evidence was found of Animoca holding a board seat at YGG, which operates as a DAO structure.

## gumi Cryptos Capital and Qredo investment details

### gCC investment in Qredo — lead investor status confirmed

gumi Cryptos Capital was a **lead investor** in Qredo across two rounds:

**Pre-Seed Round (March 2019)**
- Total round: $2.9M
- Role: gCC led this round
- Documentation: Qredo official blog "The Qredo Story"

**Seed Round (May 3, 2021)**
- Total round: $11M
- Role: One of six lead investors alongside G1, Maven 11, Spartan Group, 1kx, and Kenetic
- Documentation: Qredo official press release; BusinessWire; Crunchbase

Miko Matsumura's public bios consistently list Qredo among gCC's "unicorn" investments alongside OpenSea, YGG, and 1inch.

### Animoca Brands and Qredo — no investment found

**No evidence was found** of Animoca Brands investing in Qredo. Qredo's documented investor lists across all funding rounds (seed through $80M Series A) do not include Animoca Brands or Animoca Ventures.

### Qredo's current status and Zenrock relationship

Qredo entered UK administration on February 2, 2024 following multiple layoffs and leadership changes. The company's assets were acquired by Fusion Laboratories Ltd (backed by 10T Holdings and 1RoundTable Partners), which rebranded as **Zenrock** in March 2024.

Zenrock's documented backers are 10T Holdings, 1RoundTable Partners, Maven 11, and Spartan Group. **No evidence was found** of gumi Cryptos Capital investing in Zenrock.

## Cross-holdings and board seats

**No direct cross-holdings were found** between gumi Cryptos Capital and Animoca Brands (i.e., neither firm owns equity in the other).

The only structural relationship identified is through the **Decima Fund**, where gumi Inc. (gCC's parent) and Animoca Brands serve as co-General Partners.

**No board seats** were found where gCC partners serve on Animoca-controlled companies, or where Animoca executives serve on gCC portfolio company boards due to gCC's involvement.
